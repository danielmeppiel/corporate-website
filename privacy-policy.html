Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Corporate Inc. ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

2. Data We Collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the following information:

  • Name: To address you properly in our communications
  • Email address: To respond to your inquiry
  • Message content: To understand and respond to your request
  • Timestamp: For record-keeping and audit purposes

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent, which you provide by checking the privacy policy consent checkbox when submitting the contact form.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your inquiries and requests
  • Providing information about our services
  • Maintaining audit logs for compliance purposes

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for a maximum of 5 years, after which they are automatically deleted. Audit logs are retained for 7 years as required by legal obligations.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: You can request deletion of your data
  • Right to data portability: You can request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object: You can object to processing of your data
  • Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw consent at any time

7. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Our cookie consent banner allows you to control which cookies are set. We use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics cookies: To understand how visitors use our site (only with your consent)
  • Marketing cookies: To deliver relevant content (only with your consent)

8. reCAPTCHA

We use Google reCAPTCHA to protect our contact form from spam and abuse. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information and sends it to Google for analysis. This is necessary for the security of our website. For more information, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Staff training on data protection

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed within the European Economic Area (EEA). If data is transferred outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

12. Changes to This Policy

13. Supervisory Authority
